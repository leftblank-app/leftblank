LeftBlank adaptation

The bundled guide adapts the explanatory-illustration workflow, visual
conventions, and Xiaohei character concept of:
https://github.com/helloianneo/ian-xiaohei-illustrations
Pinned reference: 8961915fcdbca57f288ec52b173056501d8a87f2

The upstream skill targets article illustrations. This adaptation also supports
illustrations sized for presentation use. It supplies newly written editable
native Typst geometry and original demonstration scenes, rather than the
upstream image-generation workflow. No upstream example images are bundled.
